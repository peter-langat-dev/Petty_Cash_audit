# Petty_Cash_audit 

##### Project title : Petty_cash_audit
##### Organization : "Confidential Hardware"
##### Location : Operations, Main Shop
##### Audit fieldwork dates : 2-6 June 2026
##### Period reviewed : 1 January - 30 May 2025 
##### Auditor : Peter Langat
##### Report date : 15 June 2026

# Objective
##### This is a audit on Petty Cash to verify transactions, examine supporting documentation and test controls put by management if they are followed by Petty Cash staff. 
#### The objective of this audit was to determine whether the Petty cash is:
##### • Properly authorized
##### • Safely maintained
##### • Supported by valid receipts
##### • Reconciled to the cash balance

# Audit steps
##### • Obtained the petty cash policy and approved fund amount
##### • Count the physical cash, when the custodian (Cashier) was present.
##### • Inspected receipts and vouchers for authorization, dates, descriptions and signatures.
##### • Recalculated  the total:
  #####   Cash on hand + Valid vouchers = Approved petty cash float.
##### • Identified missing receipts, duplicate claims, unsupported expenses and old outstanding vouchers.
##### • I compared selected transactions with the accounting records.
##### • I documented the differences and discussed them with the supervisor.
##### • I prepared a short report with findings and recomendations.

# Findings
##### • Finding : Three Petty Cash payments lacked evidence of supervisory approval.

##### • Risk : Unauthorized or personal expenses may be reimbursed.

##### • Recommendation: Require the custodian to attach an approved petty cash request form and supporting receipt before payment.
